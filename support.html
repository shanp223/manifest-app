Frequently asked questions

How do I start a trip?

Tap New, type where you're going and pick your dates. Add trip types such as Beach or Business, and anything you already know you'll forget, then tap Build checklist. Manifest writes a packing list for the trip.

How does the boarding-pass scan work?

Tap Scan and take a photo of a boarding pass, or pick an airline app screenshot or confirmation email from your photos. Manifest reads the barcode and text on your iPhone and fills in the flight, airports, date, seat and more. Give the details a quick check. The photo is never uploaded.

How are packing lists written?

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence turned on, Apple's on-device model writes a list for your destination and trip types. Otherwise Manifest uses its own built-in list. You can add, remove and reorganise items any time.

How do I check items off?

Tap an item to check it and tap again to uncheck it. Uncheck all resets the list, and Edit lets you remove items. Changes sync to your other devices.

How do I add an item by voice?

On the Trips screen, press and hold the big packing ring and say the item, for example "phone charger". You can also tap the microphone next to the text box.

What are Gaps and Retro?

After a trip, open Retro and swipe through what you packed: used it or dead weight. Manifest learns your baseline from that. Gaps then flags things you've packed on three or more past trips but haven't added to the current one.

Does Manifest show live flight status?

On travel days the flight card has a Check button that searches for your flight's latest status. Always confirm gates and times with your airline.

What's Simple mode?

The Simple switch at the top hides the weather, gaps, trip types and hints, and keeps trips, the checklist and your flight.

How do I delete my account?

On the Trips screen, tap Out, then Delete account… and confirm. This permanently deletes your account and all your trips right away.

My trips aren't showing up

Make sure you signed in the same way as before (Apple or Google, with the same account). Trips made with one don't appear under the other. Then check that you're online, then pull down on the Trips screen to refresh.

Does Manifest cost anything?

No. Manifest is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Accessibility

We want Manifest to work for as many people as possible. Right now it includes:

  • VoiceOver labels on buttons, checklist items (with packed / not packed state), trip cards and menus. The retro cards have "Used it" and "Dead weight" actions, so no swiping is needed.
  • Packing progress is always shown as numbers (for example 12/30) as well as colour, and packed items get a checkmark and strike-through, not just a colour change.
  • Text follows the iOS Larger Text (Dynamic Type) setting, including the larger accessibility sizes. A new size takes effect the next time you open Manifest.
  • When Reduce Motion is on, list, card and screen animations are turned off.
  • Text colours meet the WCAG 4.5:1 contrast guideline for normal text in both themes.
  • Light and dark themes.
  • Large touch targets for checklist rows and main buttons.
  • Simple mode, which removes secondary information and enlarges the checklist.
  • Everything you can do by voice or by holding the ring can also be done by tapping and typing.

If anything in Manifest is hard for you to use, email pixxlpickle@gmail.com with "Accessibility" in the subject line. We read every message, respond within 2 business days, and will work with you on a reasonable accommodation.
