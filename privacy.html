Privacy Policy — Manifest

Effective date: September 18, 2026
Contact: pixxlpickle@gmail.com

Summary

Manifest is a packing-list app for iPhone. You sign in with Apple or Google so your trips sync between your devices, and we store your account and the trips you create. Packing lists are written and boarding passes are read on your iPhone. Your photos never leave your device. We do not sell your data, show ads, use analytics, or track you across other apps and websites.

What we collect and why

  • Account details. When you sign in with Apple, we receive your email address (or a private relay address if you choose Hide My Email) and, the first time only, your name if you share it. When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and name (and Google may include your profile picture). We use them only to create your account and sign you in. We never see your Apple or Google password.
  • Your trips. Destination, dates, trip types and flight details (airline, flight number, airports, times, gate, terminal, seat and booking reference), your packing items and what you've checked off, trip retros, your packing history and items you've dismissed. We store these so they appear on all your devices.
  • A user ID that links your trips to your account.

We do not collect your location, contacts, health data, browsing history, or advertising identifiers.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Photos you scan. When you scan a boarding pass, screenshot or confirmation email, the image is read on your device using Apple's Vision framework and, when available, Apple's on-device language model. The image is never uploaded or stored. Only the flight details you choose to save are added to your trip.
  • Packing-list suggestions are generated on your device by Apple's on-device language model, or from a built-in list if it isn't available.
  • Voice capture. When you add an item by voice, speech is turned into text by iOS on your device. We never receive the audio.
  • Preferences such as light or dark theme and Simple mode.

The app uses the camera, photo library, microphone and speech recognition only when you tap to use them, and only after iOS asks for your permission.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase stores your account and trips, and handles sign-in.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, and Google provides Sign in with Google.
  • Open-Meteo receives your trip's destination name to show a weather forecast. It does not receive your name, email or account.
  • Apple also distributes the app. On-device processing described above does not send your data to Apple.

If you tap Check for live flight status, your browser opens a web search for that flight number; that search is governed by the search provider's privacy policy. We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Security and retention

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The database only lets a signed-in user read their own records. We keep your data until you delete it or your account.

Deleting your data

  • A trip: delete it from your trip list or the trip screen. This permanently removes the trip and its packing items.
  • Your account: tap Out → Delete account… in the app. This permanently deletes your account and all of its trips, lists and history right away. You can also email pixxlpickle@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

Manifest is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict how it is used. Email us to make a request; we will respond within 30 days and will not treat you differently for using these rights. Your data may be processed in the United States and other countries where our providers operate.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Manifest handles data, we will update this policy and its effective date, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app.
