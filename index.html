Manifest

The packing list that learns from every trip. Snap your boarding pass and it handles the flight details too.

  • Packing lists built for your destination, trip length and trip type
  • Scan a boarding pass to fill in your flight, read right on your iPhone
  • Boarding and leave-home times, worked back from your flight
  • A checklist grouped by night before, morning of and day of travel, synced across your devices
  • Gap check flags what you usually pack but forgot this time
  • Trip retros teach Manifest what you actually use

Manifest is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
